# Surveillance frameworks and series codes

Exchanges publish the current lists daily (NSE: "Surveillance" pages; BSE: "Surveillance measures").
Criteria change by circular. The descriptions here explain *what it means for a trader*.

## ASM — Additional Surveillance Measure
- **Why:** abnormal price rise or fall, high volatility, client concentration, or price-volume
  variation relative to fundamentals.
- **Types:** long-term ASM (stages I–IV) and short-term ASM (stages I–II).
- **Effect:** higher margins (50% → 100% at higher stages). Some stages move the stock to
  trade-for-trade settlement or tighter price bands.
- **Trader impact:** leverage disappears, intraday becomes expensive, and liquidity often dries up.
  Treat entry into ASM as a warning about speculative activity.

## GSM — Graded Surveillance Measure
- **Why:** low price and market cap that are not commensurate with fundamentals (shell-company risk).
- **Stages 0–IV:** trade-for-trade, then an additional surveillance deposit, then trading allowed only
  once a week (with deposit) at the highest stage.
- **Trader impact:** exits can take weeks. Avoid.

## ESM — Enhanced Surveillance Measure
- **Scope:** micro and small companies (market cap below ₹500 crore) with price variation above thresholds.
- **Stage I:** 5% (or 2%) price band, trade-for-trade, 100% margin.
- **Stage II:** 2% band and periodic call auction (trading only in auction windows).

## Other flags
- **IBC (insolvency) stocks** and companies with **auditor resignations** or **pledge invocations**
  often move into tighter bands.
- **Unsolicited-tip stocks:** SEBI and exchanges warn about stocks promoted over SMS, Telegram or
  WhatsApp. Price bands may be cut to 2% or 5%.

## Series codes (NSE)
| Series | Meaning |
|---|---|
| EQ | Normal rolling settlement; intraday allowed |
| BE | Trade-for-trade: every trade settles by delivery, no intraday squaring |
| BZ | Trade-for-trade for companies not complying with listing rules |
| SM / ST | SME platform (NSE Emerge): large lots, thinner liquidity |
| GB | Sovereign Gold Bonds |
| N*, Y* etc. | Debt instruments (bonds, NCDs) |

BSE groups: **A** (most liquid), **B**, **T** (trade-for-trade), **X/XT** (smaller), **Z**
(non-compliant), **M/MT** (SME).

## Practical checks before any trade
1. Series is EQ (NSE) or A/B group (BSE) for normal trading.
2. The stock is not in ASM/GSM/ESM. Check the exchange surveillance list or the broker's warning badge.
3. The price band is known (2/5/10/20%). A 2% band means circuits are easy to hit.
4. Average daily traded value is enough for your size. Keep position size under about 1% of average
   daily value in small caps.
