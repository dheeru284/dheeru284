# Regulatory-readiness checklist (for review by qualified Indian legal counsel)

**Nothing here is legal advice or a compliance claim.** Each item is a question to resolve. The admin "Regulatory readiness" registry tracks status per jurisdiction; items can block bookings in a state until satisfied or a documented exception is recorded.

Key open question: whether operating a nurse-booking marketplace requires registration as a clinical establishment (or similar) in each state — it depends on the actual operating model (who employs/contracts nurses, who sets clinical protocols, who is liable), legal structure, and the state/UT framework. Do not assume either answer.

## 1. Platform operator
- [ ] DPDP Act 2023 + DPDP Rules: notice, purpose-specific consent, consent withdrawal, rights requests, grievance officer, breach notification, retention, processor contracts; Significant Data Fiduciary assessment (health data)
- [ ] IT Act 2000, SPDI Rules 2011 (to extent still applicable), Intermediary Rules 2021 (grievance, due diligence)
- [ ] CERT-In Directions 2022: 6-hour incident reporting, 180-day log retention, NTP sync
- [ ] Clinical establishment / private medical establishment registration — state by state
- [ ] Telemedicine Practice Guidelines — confirm out of scope (no teleconsultation offered)
- [ ] Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020
- [ ] GST registration; tax on commission vs. healthcare-service exemptions
- [ ] Shops & Establishments registration; company/LLP compliance
- [ ] Professional indemnity / general liability insurance
- [ ] Final privacy policy, terms, refund policy (current pages are drafts)
- [ ] Emergency messaging wording reviewed

## 2. Nurses
- [ ] Valid State Nursing Council registration in the state of practice; inter-state practice rules; renewal cycles
- [ ] Indian Nursing Council qualification recognition (ANM/GNM/B.Sc etc.) and scope of practice per service
- [ ] Contract model (independent contractor vs. employee) — labour code implications
- [ ] Police/background verification policy
- [ ] Individual indemnity cover

## 3. Clinical establishments / healthcare providers
- [ ] Clinical protocols per service signed off by a qualified clinician (seeded checklists are drafts)
- [ ] Prescription/clinician-order validation process for injections, IV, catheter, medication
- [ ] Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016 — who is the generator for home visits; disposal arrangement
- [ ] Clinical record retention period

## 4. Diagnostic / laboratory partners
- [ ] Lab registration under state rules; NABL accreditation (voluntary) ; sample transport and chain of custody
- [ ] Blood sample collection stays disabled until a partner agreement exists

## 5. Accommodation operators
- [ ] Local trade/municipal licences, fire NOC, police guest reporting (Form C for foreign nationals)
- [ ] GST on accommodation; platform's TCS/TDS obligations as an e-commerce operator

## 6. Payment & technology providers
- [ ] Payment Aggregator model and settlement flows (RBI PA guidelines); escrow/nodal arrangement for marketplace splits
- [ ] DPAs with SMS, maps, storage, payment processors; data localisation requirements for payment data
- [ ] SMS DLT registration (TRAI) for templates and sender IDs

## 7. Future: medicine delivery
- [ ] Drugs and Cosmetics Act/Rules, pharmacy licensing, registered pharmacist, e-pharmacy rules status, Schedule H/H1/X restrictions, cold chain — feature locked off
